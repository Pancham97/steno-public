How it works

Hold a key, talk, and paste.

01

Hold the Fn key

Press and hold the Fn key in any app. A small Steno window opens.

02

Talk

Say what you want to write. Steno turns it into clean, punctuated text.

03

Paste

Steno copies the text. Paste it into Mail, Notes, Messages, or any other app.

Profiles

Your words, in the shape you need.

Steno doesn't guess how your words should look — you decide. Create a profile with your own instructions, and Steno reshapes what you say to match: one for support replies, one for standup updates, or whatever you write most.
