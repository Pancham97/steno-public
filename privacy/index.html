Privacy Policy

Your data stays on your Mac.

Steno only records while you dictate. It turns your speech into text on your Mac, and keeps your audio, transcripts, profiles, and diagnostics on your Mac unless you choose to share them.

Runs on your Mac
Dictation and Profile Rewrite run on your Mac, using models that come with the app.
No account
Steno does not need a sign-in or an online account to work.
No tracking
Steno does not collect analytics, crash reports, your audio, your transcripts, your clipboard, writing profiles, or diagnostic exports.

What Steno may access

Microphone
To record your voice only while you hold the dictation key.
Clipboard
By default, Steno copies finished transcripts to the clipboard so you can paste them into another app yourself. You can turn off "Copy transcript to clipboard after dictation" if you want Steno to leave the clipboard unchanged. When clipboard saving is off, completed transcripts can still be saved in Transcript Library if history is enabled.

What stays local

Transcripts, settings, and profiles
Saved transcripts, settings, writing profiles, and Profile Rewrite output stay on your Mac.
Temporary audio
Temporary local audio files used during capture are removed when transcription finishes or when capture is canceled.
Bundled models
The app comes with the speech-to-text and Profile Rewrite models it needs. Steno does not send your audio or transcripts to a server.
Transcript history
History is on by default and stored only on your Mac. You can turn it off, choose a retention period, pin transcripts you want to keep, or delete all history.
Diagnostics
Diagnostics exports are local files and are shared only if you choose to send them. Diagnostics do not include dictated text, audio, or clipboard contents.

Sharing and contact

Copying, sharing, exporting, and pasting are actions you start. Once content leaves Steno, it is governed by the app or service you chose.
