Steno Privacy Policy
Last updated April 13, 2026

Mostly local. No account. No tracking.

Steno is a local-first dictation app for macOS, iPhone, and iPad. Your audio, transcripts, and edits stay on your device by default. This page explains what the app can access, when it uses the network, and when content leaves the app because you asked it to.

In short

  • Steno does not require an account.
  • Steno does not use ad trackers or third-party analytics SDKs.
  • Audio and transcript content are processed locally by default.
  • Handoff shares resume metadata, not full transcript text.

What Steno may access

  • Microphone to record speech you want to transcribe.
  • Speech recognition when Apple requires it for local transcription features.
  • Accessibility on macOS when you use Steno to insert text into another app.
  • Clipboard when you copy text, or when the macOS insert workflow uses the clipboard to paste into another app.

What stays local

Saved transcripts, settings, and writing profiles are stored locally on your device. During capture, Steno may create a temporary local audio file so the device can complete transcription. That file is removed when transcription finishes or when capture is canceled.

Steno does not send your microphone audio, transcript text, or refined output to a developer cloud for transcription or rewriting.

When the network is used

Your device may download speech or refine models so they can run locally. Those downloads are for local feature delivery, not remote processing.

Steno does not upload your microphone audio or saved transcript text for advertising, profiling, or cloud transcription.

Sharing, Handoff, and diagnostics

Copying, sharing, exporting, and pasting are actions you start. Once content leaves Steno, it is governed by the app or service you send it to.

Handoff can share resume metadata such as a transcript identifier, timing, and where to continue. It does not automatically share the full transcript body.

Diagnostics exports are written to a local file and are only shared if you choose to send them. They are designed to include runtime state, model status, error codes, and latency information, while excluding transcript text, clipboard contents, and raw-audio references.

Your control

You can delete saved transcripts in the app, remove downloaded models where those controls are available, or delete the app and its local data from your device.

Because Steno does not depend on a developer account for core transcription, the developer generally cannot remotely recover or delete your local transcripts for you.

Changes and contact

This page may be updated if the product changes. The current version will always be posted here with its effective date.
