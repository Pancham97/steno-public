Privacy Policy

Private by default.

Steno keeps audio and text on your device by default. This page covers the short version.

Local-first

Audio and transcript processing stay on your device by default.

No account

Steno does not require sign-in for core transcription use.

No tracking

Steno does not use ad trackers or third-party analytics SDKs.

What Steno may access

  • Microphone To record speech you want to transcribe.
  • Speech recognition When Apple requires it for local transcription features.
  • Accessibility on macOS When you use Steno to insert text into another app.
  • Clipboard When you copy text, or when the macOS insert workflow uses the clipboard to paste text.

What stays local

  • Transcripts and settings Saved transcripts, settings, and writing profiles stay on your device.
  • Temporary audio Temporary local audio files used during capture are removed when transcription finishes or when capture is canceled.
  • Model downloads If your device downloads models, they are downloaded so features can run locally.
  • Handoff Handoff can share resume metadata, not the full transcript body.
  • Diagnostics Diagnostics exports are local files and are shared only if you choose to send them.

Sharing and contact

Copying, sharing, exporting, and pasting are actions you start. Once content leaves Steno, it is governed by the app or service you chose.
