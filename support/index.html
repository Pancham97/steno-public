Support

Help for Steno.

For support, bug reports, and general questions, email hello@panchamkhaitan.com.

Include this in your email

  • Device: Mac, iPhone, or iPad.
  • App version and OS version.
  • What you expected to happen.
  • What happened instead.

Common checks

  • Confirm Microphone access is enabled.
  • Confirm any Apple Speech Recognition permission the platform requires.
  • On macOS, confirm Accessibility access if text insertion is not working.
