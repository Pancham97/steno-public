Privacy Policy

Support for Steno.

For support, bug reports, and general questions, email hello@panchamkhaitan.com.

Include this in your email

  • What device you are using: Mac, iPhone, or iPad.
  • Your app version and OS version.
  • What you expected to happen.
  • What happened instead, and how to reproduce it.

Common setup checks

  • If dictation is not starting, confirm Microphone access is enabled for Steno.
  • If transcription is unavailable, confirm any Apple Speech Recognition permission the platform requires.
  • If text insertion on macOS is not working, confirm Accessibility access is enabled for Steno.

Bug reports

If you are reporting a bug, include the steps that led to the problem and whether it happens every time or only sometimes. Screenshots are helpful when the issue is visual.
